abstract: |
  In a result due to Alaghmandan and Spronk, it was shown for a compact group \(G\), that if \(G\) is virtually abelian (that is, has an abelian subgroup of finite index), then a certain algebra '\(ZA(G)\)' is amenable.
  It is conjectured that these conditions are equivalent.
  We show a result that the p-adic group \(\mathbb{O}_p\) admits an algebra which is not amenable.
  This algebra can be identified with a quotient of \(ZA(\mathbb{O}_p \rtimes \mathbb{O}^∗_p)\), which further reinforces the conjecture.
